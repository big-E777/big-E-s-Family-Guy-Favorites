Top 5 Favorite Family Guy Characters

HM. Carter Pewterschmidt

Lois's father, and a classic recurring character, Carter Pewterschmidt takes the honorable mention on my list. He follows the classic rich grandparent, sometimes even being comically evil, such as the time he had a cure for cancer and kept it for only himself. The episodes he's in are always great, and his jokes land. There have been some jokes of his that have gained internet popularity and even became a meme, like when they parodied Jamiroquai, specifically virtual insanity, with Carter moving furniture and even letting his bird outside of his cage. Not every epsiodes focusing around him has been a favorite of mine though, I remember not caring too much for the episode where he has an affair, this defientely knocked him down some points. Otherwise he has an iconic voice and is an overall, funny character, they sadly just don't use him enough, meaning I can't put him any higher.

5. Mayor Adam West

Taking the number 5 spot on my list is the old Mayor of Quahog Adam West. This guy has the funniest moments in Family Guy. He was by far the funniest character and it was always a treat when he graced the episode with his presence. He was sadly quite underutilized and very sadly, his voice actor who was named Adam West and played Batman in various media passed away in 2017. Since Mayor Adam West was a portrayal and basically just this Actor, they decided that he died in the show too and Quahog had to get a new mayor. The new mayor is no where near as good as Mayor Adam West. A lot of his jokes were random and absurd and just so funny. He investigated who was stealing his tap water when it was just going down the drain, he built a gold statue of a cereal mascot, and he handed chicken on halloween to trick or treaters. these are just some examples of the great jokes he had. RIP Adam West.

4. Chris Griffin

Where do I even begin? Peter Griffin's son, with his iconic voice coming from seth green, creator of another popular adult American TV show, Robot Chicken. Chris is definetely one of the funniest Family Guy characters, but he defientely has a couple jokes that didn't hit at all, so sadly, I can't put him any higher than number four. You also have to be in a Chris mood to really enjoy him. He's the classic idiot, making dumb decisions, doing dumb things, I mean you can't not laugh at this guy. He's defientey had his viral moments on the internet, with his most recent viral joke being one I and many do not like, as it became popular because it was so bad. That joke was when the police came in to arrest Chris and they did a tiktok dance, yeah, pretty bad. Other than that he's a great character, I love the episode "Stewie, Chris, & Brian's Excellent Adventure" where Stewie and Brian uses his time machine to help Chris study for his history test. Another great episode which also had an image in it become quite popular online is the episode "Dead Dog Walking" with a clip where Lois tells Chris he looks like a douchebag when smoking, I sadly can't show the image.

3. Peter Griffin

On the podium taking bronze home... The iconic, classic, and the Family Guy himself, Peter Griffin takes my number 3 spot. Theres not much to say, truly a staple of American TV, up there with the likes of Homer, Hank Hill, Bob Belcher (I'd say better than all of them). What's there not to love, great character design, funny, dumb. chris defientely got his idiot genes from this guy, Peter is definetely not as dumb as Chris but hes had his moments. He has some of the funniest jokes on Family Guy, with quite a good amount of ones popular online. A great example of a peter joke that was great and went viral online was when he had an evil twin named Retep, who wore a green shirt and white pants. So absurd and random it's funny. I love the scenes where he's at The Clam with his buddies, Quagmire, Cleveland, and Joe, they aren't included in this list but they definetely take the 6, and 7, spot respectively (with one of them being higher on this list). Peter's a great charcater, of a great TV show. I didn't put him any higher as with being in almost every episode of a comedic show, not all of your jokes are gonna land and be funny, this has definetely happened with peter, even having whole epsiodes where the jokes don't really land.

2. Joe Swanson

On the podium, taking silver home... An Icon in the Family Guy show, Joe Swanson. The cool, tough cop who lost feeling and control in his legs whne he got shot. Joe is such a funny charcter and a necesity in this show.He's one of Peter's dinking buddies at The Clam and sadly the butt of a lot of jokes. Joe Swanson would've taken the number one spot any day of the week in early Family Guy. He was an epic cop, who was tough and showed how strong he was, even in a wheelchair. But that all changed as the show went on... They started to make him a loser, BECAUSE he was in a wheelchair. They make fun of him, his wife hates him. It's horrible, I hate when they're mean to Joe and I also don't understand why they made him such a loser. They had a perfect charcter, with some of the only wheelchair representation in adult animated TV, and what did they do with it? Turned him into a loser. Even with all of that it's undeniable those early days were great, and he isn't bad as a character today, I like like the character he portrays today. But why did they have to do it to Joe, poor Joe. One of the most popular scenes online of his is when he tells Peter to do a "Leroy Jenkins" (another popular internet meme). At this point in time Leroy Jenkins was very old and not funny so they all give Joe a cold stare until Quagmire said Don't worry I got this, What did he do? He wheeled Joe into the corner and put a broom behind his wheelchair so he couldn't move, Joe then looked down, sadly. What the hell. that is too far and a great example of what they made this character into. People in comment sections can agree that they make fun of Joe way too much with people saying things like "when they're mean to Joe on Family Guy" With a picture of symbiote Spiderman looking down sadly on the corner of a building in the rain. Other than all of that Joe is super funny, even sometimes in modern Family Guy and he definetely could've taken the number one spot.

1. Stewie Grifin

On the podium taking gold home... The best character in Family Guy goes to Stewie Griffin. This is an example of a charcter that changed over time but in a way that helped and didn't hurt the character like Joe's changes did. Stewie Griffin has some of the greastest moments and episodes. Stewie is commonly seen with Brian Griffin going on adventures. These adventures can be anywhere at any time as Stewie has a time machine wich always leads to great episodes and moments. Stewie started as genius evil baby who spoke but no one could understand. His one and only goal was to kill Lois which was pretty funny but it was getting pretty old, so what did they do? They changed him and I would say it made the character better. He started hanging out with Brian and did other things than just try to kill Lois. A great episode and example of the advenutres Stewie and Brian go on is the episode "Back to the Pilot" where Stewie and Brian go back to the pilot episode and make a change that destroys the world so a bunch of Stewies and Brians go back in time to the same point to eithr stop them from making the change or tell them to make the change. A great character who defientely deserves the number one spot.
