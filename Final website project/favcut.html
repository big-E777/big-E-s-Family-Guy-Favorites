Top Five Favorite Family Guy Cutaways

HM. Peter's Karaoke Phase

An honorable mention goes out to Peter's Karaoke Phase. This joke I don't see talked about too often and I see it be overlooked but it's pretty dang funny. This joke starts withs Lois wondering who threw up in the sink and didn't clean it up. She turns around to find peter with a micophone and karaoke machine in the counter playing "Baba O'Riley" by the band, The Who. This song notobly, has a very long intro leading to Peter making the audience and Lois wait through the whole intro, I believe being around a minute. After the intro Peter proceeds to sing one line of the song, before quickly saying "yeah that was me I threw up in the sink" and then continuing to sing the song. A truly funny joke that was definetely used to help the epsiode reach its runtime, that deserves the honorable mention and almost made my top five.

This chicken hates Peter with all of his heart and Peter ahtes this chicken with all of his heart. It's a classic reaccuring gag within the show that's loved by many fans but was sadly ended.
